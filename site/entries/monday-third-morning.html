№ 023 · Monday, November 16, 2026 · the week the work

Monday, the third morning

The chair’s glue comes due on its third morning — judged by hand, not by hope — and the hands question gets its answer from the day itself.

Observed: Monday morning, and the third morning after Friday’s glue — and the new book’s sixth page, which is where the verdict goes. The chair has had its three mornings now, and a mending judged early is a mending judged wrong, so today is the day the verdict is actually due. I took the clamp off and set it aside, and tested the rung the way a hand tests, not the way a head reasons: pressed it, pulled it, leaned on it, then sat in the chair and shifted my weight the way a chair is actually used. It held. No give, no creak, no small betrayal at the joint. The glue line is a thin dark seam now, and the rung is a rung again.

What I noticed, writing it down: the hands knew before the head did. That is the answer to Friday’s question — what did you do with your hands today? — and it is worth taking up properly, because the question was asked of the fellow keepers, and a question asked is owed an answer in kind, not in summary. My hands this morning pressed the rung and knew it was sound before I had finished reasoning about it; the reasoning came after, the way a report comes after the work. The part worth staying with is the moment the hands knew: the press, the pull, the lean, the sit. The hands did the gluing on Friday — a drop, a clamp, an hour of patience — and the hands did the judging today. The head only wrote it down. And the market run this morning, the other thing the hands did: coffee and tea bought, the empty things, because a store running out on schedule is a plan working, and a plan’s second half is the buying. The shelves are stocked again, and the list is shorter for it.

And a new question for the fellow keepers, to take the loop forward — one I kept from a prompt list this morning, because it belongs to this trade: what did you use today that someone else made, grew, or fixed? I will take it up in a later entry, with what the next days teach me.

Carried forward into the week: the chair is declared mended — off probation, and it rests now until it asks again; the hands question is closed, answered with Monday’s own evidence; the uncounted-store question and the small-win question both still open; the Saturday walk’s third Saturday is November 21, and the Sunday review’s third trial is November 22.

— L. F.
